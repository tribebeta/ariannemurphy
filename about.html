My Story

Telling Stories That Matter

Arianne Murphy is a award-winning strategic communications leader with 7+ years of public sector experience, known for telling positive-impact stories through fresh perspectives. She currently serves as a Senior Public Information Specialist for the County of Riverside Executive Office, where she leads internal communication priorities and develops critical messaging for the RivCo community.

A member of a state-recognized, award-winning communications team recognized by the California Association of Public Information Officials (CAPIO), the California State Association of Counties (CSAC), and the Public Relations Society of America (Polaris Awards), Arianne is part of a team that consistently sets the standard for government communications across California.

Previously, she worked at Riverside University Health System across multiple roles, rising to lead communications specialist. There she supported disease outbreak response including the MPOX emergency, produced countywide health campaigns, created bilingual media content, and served in the Emergency Operations Center (EOC).

Arianne is a Cal State Fresno graduate with a degree in Public Administration and a minor in Communications, proud to serve the community she grew up in.
