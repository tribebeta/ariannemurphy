Internal Communications · Quarterly Publication

The Equity Lens

County of Riverside Executive Office · 2024
01

Arianne co-created The Equity Lens — a quarterly bilingual internal newsletter launched in direct response to a 2023 staff survey in which 39% of 7,000 respondents cited internal communications on equity as a top priority. The publication was designed as a magazine-quality piece connecting 25,000 county employees to DEIA efforts, employee stories, and departmental equity wins. Each edition was published in both English and Spanish and tied to the County's Emmy award-winning DEIA Stories video series.

Three editions published in 2024 (April, August, November) — each reaching all 25,000 employees plus 10,000 external subscribers
Last two editions surpassed the 11,000 open-rate goal, reaching 13,000+ opens each
Gained distribution support from two departments — Public Health Equity (1,300 recipients) and DPSS (6,000 employees)
Each edition featured a different county employee including a deputy director, assistant CEO, and department director
Produced using Adobe Photoshop, Microsoft Word, Canon R6 Mark II, and studio lighting

Brand / Rebrand · Internal Communications

RivCoONE Internal Brand Launch

County of Riverside Executive Office · 2025
02

Riverside County is home to 2.4M+ residents and operates 40+ departments. RivCoONE was created to shift the county from program-centered to person-centered service — a fundamental change in how the county does business. Arianne served as a key communications lead, helping develop and roll out the brand internally to 23,500+ employees before a 2026 countywide public launch. This included a key messaging deck, internal communications plan, and Graphic Standards Manual — developed with Westbound Communications.

100% adoption of the RivCoONE key messaging deck by Leadership Team, Strategy Council, and Policy Council by December 2025
Internal communications plan finalized and published, actively guiding 2026 deliverables across all communications teams
Graphic Standards Manual finalized and deployed — ensuring all new materials align to brand standards before the 2026 launch
15+ branded collateral pieces produced including narrative, newsletter template, PowerPoint templates, FAQs, and design graphics
Materials hosted on the RivCoONE intranet accessible to all 23,500+ employees
Participated in two communications-focused Rapid Improvement Events (RIEs) and stakeholder scoping sessions

Civic Engagement Campaign · Community Relations

County Budget Civic Engagement Campaign

County of Riverside Executive Office · Dec 2024 – Jun 2025
03

During the 2024 budget hearings, residents found the process hard to follow and participation was low — the highest public comment count over three years was only 17. The Executive Office launched an eight-month civic engagement campaign including a bilingual community survey, five in-district budget workshops, a Budget 101 resource guide, and a robust social media strategy. Arianne contributed to communications planning, content creation, news releases, social media, and bilingual materials.

23,840 survey responses received — goal was 10,000 (138% over target)
654,584 GovDelivery email recipients with 98% delivery rate and 391,405 unique opens
Five budget workshops with 190 in-person attendees and ~19,000 telephone town hall callers
Budget 101 resource guide distributed to 1,759 people — 75.9% over combined target of 1,000
11 earned media stories secured across The Press-Enterprise, K-News, News Channel 3, IE Business Daily, La Poderosa
More than 975 survey responses in Spanish; 125 Spanish Budget 101 guides distributed
