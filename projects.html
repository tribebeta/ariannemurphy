AI-Augmented Campaign · Internal Communications

RivCoONE Internal Launch Campaign

County of Riverside Executive Office · 2024–2025
01

Arianne invented and led the RivCoONE internal communications strategy from the ground up — a countywide culture change initiative requiring buy-in from 23,000 employees before a 2026 public launch. Using Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, and GovDelivery AI, she architected an 8-week drip campaign branded RivCoONE Wednesdays, producing 4 newsletters, 4 call-to-action bulletins, 5 campaign videos, and the CEO launch email — all automated through AI-powered scheduling.

36,212 total video views across 5 AI-scripted campaign videos
Board of Supervisors video — all 5 supervisors — became highest-performing asset with 10,145 views

Internal Communications · Quarterly Publication

02

Launched in response to a 2023 staff survey in which 39% of respondents cited internal equity communications as a top priority. Each edition was published in English and Spanish.
